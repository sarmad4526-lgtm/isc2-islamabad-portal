Would you like to hear about the ISC2 Islamabad Chapter?

Joining our member list gives you exclusive first access to our events and news before anyone else.

The ISC2 Islamabad Chapter's mission is to bring together a community of Information Security Professionals and interest groups to discuss current trends, share knowledge and drive innovation and inclusion within communities. Islamabad is a growing hub for technology, government, financial services, and telecommunications, and as such, Information Security Professionals in Islamabad have visibility of state-of-the-art security initiatives, methods and practices that are being applied globally; this wealth of knowledge should be shared for the good of the public.
